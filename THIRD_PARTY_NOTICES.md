## Model and service license gate

No model weights, datasets, or provider SDK integrations are bundled by this scaffold. Before adding any model or service, record:
- exact model/package/provider and pinned version or revision
- source URL and license terms
- commercial-use and redistribution restrictions
- attribution or notice requirements
- hardware requirements and estimated inference cost
- privacy/data-retention terms for any cloud provider

A code license does not automatically grant rights to model weights, training data, generated assets, or external APIs. Do not commit credentials, private media, or model weights without a separate reviewed decision.
